Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026. Template text — customize contact details before production use.

This policy describes how Crea Trip Planner (“the app”, “we”) handles information when you use our Telegram-based travel planning bot and related services operated by the app owner.

Information we collect

  • Telegram identity: your numeric Telegram user ID and messages you send to the bot.
  • Planning data: trips, itineraries, saved places, notes, preferences, and structured memories stored in the operator’s database.
  • Usage content: photos or documents you send for itinerary or place analysis, when you choose to share them.

How we use information

We use your data to provide travel planning, remember context across conversations, generate exports, and improve responses within your sessions. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Service providers

  • OpenAI: message content and related context may be sent to OpenAI’s API for chat, vision, and embeddings when you interact with the bot.
  • Google Maps Platform: if configured by the operator, place names or queries may be sent to Google APIs (e.g. Places, Routes, Static Maps, Weather) to enrich or answer location-related requests.
  • Telegram: messaging is delivered through Telegram’s platform, subject to Telegram’s own terms and privacy practices.

Storage and hosting

Trip data is stored in a PostgreSQL database on hardware controlled by the app operator (self-hosted). Export files (PDF/CSV) are generated on that same infrastructure. Access to the bot may be restricted to explicitly allowed Telegram user IDs.

Retention

Data is retained on the operator’s systems until deleted by the operator or as part of routine maintenance you request. There is no separate consumer account portal; contact the operator to ask about deletion.

Security

The operator is responsible for securing the server, database, API keys, and network access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; use the bot only if you accept this model.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. The operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use of the bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
